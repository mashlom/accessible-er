Ocean world ("הרפתקה תת-ימית") — pictures: sources and licence
=============================================================

STATUS: DRAFT, written 2026-10-13 from what Maria Semenova reported. Lines
marked "TO CONFIRM" have not been checked against the original sources and
must be confirmed (by Shevi, the designer, and by whoever decides on the
licence) before the app is shown outside the team.

The project rule (CLAUDE.md): every picture must be free to use, including
commercially, and its source and licence must be written down.


1. Pictures made with generative AI
-----------------------------------

  background.jpg        sea and shore background of the maps
  station-house.png     station 1, the house on the pier
  station-sub.png       station 2, the submarine
  station-pod.png       station 3 (and the stand-in for the second stage)
  station-aquarium.png  station 4, the aquarium dome with fish
  station-doctor.png    station 5, the captain's dome
  station-sonar.png     second stage, the sonar station (the X-ray and the tests)

  Tool:      Google Gemini, the free version (image generation).
             The exact model / version is not recorded.
  Made:      during the two weeks before 2026-10-13 (about 2026-09-29 to
             2026-10-13), by Shevi (designer), TO CONFIRM which pictures
             exactly, and whether all of them are Gemini's.
  Editing:   background removal (a "removebg" step, see the original file
             name of station-aquarium.png: "Gemini_Generated_Image_...
             -removebg-preview"), cropping and resizing; the pictures were
             placed in the Figma file "Mashlom.me" and exported or
             re-saved from there. TO CONFIRM the tool used for the removal.
  Prompts:   not kept. TO CONFIRM: ask Shevi to save the prompts and the
             original, unedited Gemini files (also for later, higher
             resolution versions).

  Licence / terms (TO CONFIRM — these are the open questions, not
  conclusions):

  a) Terms of use. Which Google terms applied on the day, and whether they
     allow commercial use of pictures from the FREE version. Record the
     name and date of the terms (Google Terms of Service, the Generative AI
     Additional Terms of Service, the Gemini Apps policy) and a link or a
     copy.
  b) Ownership. Whether the pictures can be protected by copyright at all;
     in several countries works made purely by AI, without enough human
     authorship, are not protected, which also means nobody can give an
     exclusive licence to them. Ask the people responsible for the project
     (ALUT, the MLR team) how this should be handled.
  c) Watermark. Gemini marks its pictures (an invisible SynthID mark, and in
     some versions a visible one). Check that nothing visible is left in
     the files and that the marks are not removed on purpose.
  d) Style resemblance. The pictures should not copy a recognizable
     character or product; Shevi to confirm that the prompts did not name
     one.
  e) Disclosure. The app should say, in the credits (README.md and the
     concept menu footer, like the Noto animations), that the ocean
     world's pictures were made with generative AI.
