# Procedure pictures: brief for the artist

The procedures (temperature, blood test, X-ray…) are shown today as thin line icons. They should look like the
pictures of the worlds: the same soft 3D clay-and-felt miniatures. One set serves all seven worlds; the world's
colour comes from the circle behind the picture, so the pictures themselves are neutral.

**What to deliver:** 18 PNG files, **512×512 px, transparent background**, named as in the table
(`procedure-<id>.png`), plus this folder's `manifest.json` (fill in the `notes` if anything differs).

**Style references:** the station pictures of any world, e.g. `public/worlds/knight/art/station-triage.webp`,
`public/worlds/dollhouse/art/station-doctor.webp`.

## Rules (for every picture)

- **One object, centred, alone.** It sits inside a circle on screen (about 64 px): keep the object within the
  central 80% of the square, nothing touching the edges.
- **The worlds' look:** rounded chunky shapes, matte felt and plasticine textures, warm soft light from the top
  left, soft shadow under the object, pastel colours, no outlines.
- **Nothing scary.** No needles, no blood, no wounds, no body parts, no people or faces. A blood test is a closed
  tube with a plaster, not a syringe.
- **No text**, no letters or numbers on the object.
- **Neutral colours** (soft white, light grey, a little pastel blue, mint or peach): the same picture is used in
  every world.

## Shared style prompt

Use this at the start of every prompt, then add the object from the table:

> Soft 3D clay-and-felt miniature, cute and calm, for a children's hospital app. Rounded chunky shapes, matte felt
> and plasticine textures, warm soft light from the top left, gentle shadow under the object, pastel colours (soft
> white, light grey, pale blue, mint, peach), no outlines. A single object, centred, three-quarter front view,
> fully inside the central 80% of a square frame. Transparent background. No text, no numbers, no people, no
> faces, no body parts, nothing sharp or scary: no needles, no blood, no wounds.

## Before sending

- Check that all 18 look like one family: same light, same softness, same scale of detail.
- Open a few on white and on a pastel circle: the object should read clearly at 64 px.
- The pictures of procedures are shown to parents and children: the team (MLR) looks at them before they go in,
  as with the texts.
